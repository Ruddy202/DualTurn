Overview

In multiparty conversation, most speech does not hand over the floor. Pauses, backchannels, brief interjections and overlap can look like transitions without being one. DualTurn splits the problem into two stages, inspired by dual-process theory.

System 1

Discover

A fast, recall-oriented process scans the audio continuously and proposes possible transition points.

→
System 2

Verify

A selective process checks each candidate with acoustic, linguistic, future-activity, speaker-change, ranking and agreement cues.

→
Output

Localize

Accepted candidates become floor-transfer events, matched one-to-one to the reference within a time tolerance.

Three evaluation tasks

A

Supplied-point classification

A decision time is given. Is the next event a SHIFT or a HOLD?

B

Candidate verification

Is an automatically proposed candidate a genuine floor transfer?

C

Continuous discovery

Find and localize floor transfers on the timeline, matched at ±0.50, ±0.70 or ±1.00 s.

Turn-taking events

VoxConverse provides diarization only. Events are derived from adjacent speaker segments using a 0.06 s overlap threshold and a 0.50 s incoming-duration threshold.

EventPatternLabel
Normal turn transfer (NTT)New speaker, no substantial overlap, speaks > 0.50 sSHIFT
Interruption (ITT)New speaker, overlap ≥ 0.06 s, speaks > 0.50 sSHIFT
